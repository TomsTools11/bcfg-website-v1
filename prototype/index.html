What changed for mobile

Right-sized photography

Gallery shots are now uniform 3:4 tiles in a snap-scrolling rail instead of 320px free-height images. The contact photo went from a 560px slab to a 3:2 frame, and Chris's portrait is capped at 430px.

Lakes collapse

Eight stacked lake cards became an accordion. Tapping a map pin, or a lake chip under the map, opens that lake and scrolls to it. All copy, acreage, and drive times are unchanged.

Hero that fits a phone

On mobile the slideshow becomes a full-bleed 58vh image with the headline set over it; on desktop it stays the original two-column layout.

Thumb-friendly booking

A fixed Call / Book bar sits at the bottom on phones, the logo shrinks from 100px to 52px, every tap target clears 44px, and form fields are 16px so iOS stops zooming on focus.
