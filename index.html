What's different on a phone

Photos you can flick through

Trip photos sit in a neat row you swipe sideways with your thumb, instead of running one after another down a very long page. Tap any photo to see it full size.

Lakes open one at a time

The eight lakes are folded up so you can see them all at a glance. Tap a lake on the map, or its name underneath, and it opens. Nothing was cut — the acreage, drive times, and photos are all still there.

An opening photo that fits

On a phone the opening photo fills the screen with the headline sitting over it. On a computer it keeps the original side-by-side layout.

Easy to call and book

A Call and Book a Trip bar stays put at the bottom of the screen, so booking is always one tap away. Every button is sized to be comfortable to hit, and the form won't zoom in on you when you start typing.
